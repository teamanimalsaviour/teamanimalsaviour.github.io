About Us

Team Animal Saviour is an independent animal rescue initiative based in Aliganj, Etah, Uttar Pradesh. Run by a single rescuer, we save injured, trapped and dangerous animals including snakes, birds, street dogs, bats and wildlife.

What We Do

  • 🐍 Snake Rescue (Cobras, Vipers, Rat Snakes)
  • 🕊 Injured Bird Rescue
  • 🐶 Street Dog Rescue
  • 🦇 Bat & Wildlife Rescue
  • 📢 Awareness & Safety Education
